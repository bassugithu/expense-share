About Expense Share

Simple expense sharing for groups, friends and shared trips.

EXPENSE SHARE APP helps groups record expenses, see who paid, understand balances and settle shared costs with less confusion.

Whether you're sharing a room, planning a trip or managing everyday group expenses, Expense Share keeps the information organized in one place.

Information we may handle

Expense Share may handle information required to provide account, group, expense and settlement features, including your name, group information, expenses and related app data.

Google Sign-In

If Google Sign-In is enabled, authentication information provided by Google may be used to identify and sign you into your Expense Share account.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide expense-sharing features, calculate balances, maintain accounts, improve security and respond to support requests.

Data storage

Expense Share may use Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Information is handled as necessary to provide the app's features.
