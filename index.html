Information we may handle

Expense Share may handle information required to provide account, group, expense and settlement features, including your name, group information, expenses and related app data.

If Google Sign-In is enabled, authentication information provided by Google may be used to identify and sign you into your Expense Share account.

Information may be used to provide expense-sharing features, calculate balances, maintain accounts, improve security and respond to support requests.
