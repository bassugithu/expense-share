Privacy Policy

How ExpenseShare handles information.

Updated 26 Oct 2026

Developer: BASAVANAGOWDA J N

Privacy contact: jybsapps@gmail.com

Account and shared group information

ExpenseShare collects your name, email address and account identifier to create and manage your account and provide app features. Email/password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. We store group names, member details, expense descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, split details, balances, settlement records, and the messages, replies, reactions and read status you choose to add to group chat.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides authentication information, including your account identifier, name and email address, to identify you and sign you into ExpenseShare. A Google profile-photo URL may be cached on your device. Profile photos selected from your device stay on that device and are not uploaded by ExpenseShare.

Optional payment details

You may save a UPI ID and phone number to help authorized group members settle payments. These details are optional. When you choose to pay, relevant payment details may be passed to your chosen payment app, whose privacy policy applies to that payment. ExpenseShare records expense and settlement information but does not collect bank passwords, card numbers or UPI PINs.

Device registration and service monitoring

We record an installation identifier, notification token, app version, platform and activity time to deliver notifications, report installed versions, monitor service activity and support app features. Notification content may include activity from your groups.

Advertising and Google AdMob

ExpenseShare displays ads using Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares device and advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, crash logs and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. ExpenseShare does not send your account email, group expenses or chat text to AdMob as ad-targeting data. You can manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

How information is used

Account and group information is used to sign you in, create and join groups, record and split expenses, calculate settlements, provide chat, keep records synchronized and respond to support requests. Group records are visible to the relevant group members. Payment details are made available to authorized members when needed for settlement. Invite codes should be shared only with people you intend to invite.

Data storage

ExpenseShare uses Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, server functions and push notifications. Data transmitted to these services and AdMob uses encrypted connections. Pending expenses and messages can be stored on your device and synchronized when internet is available. Account and group records are retained while needed to provide the service; shared records remain available to other members even after an account is removed. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Permissions and choices

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a group QR code; entering a group code manually does not require the camera. Biometric app lock is optional and is handled by Android: ExpenseShare does not receive your fingerprints or face data. You can choose whether to enter payment details, send chat messages or create expenses, and can manage notification permissions in Android settings.

Account Deletion

Request deletion of your ExpenseShare account.

Want to delete your account?
Send a request to our support email.

Email jybsapps@gmail.com with the subject ExpenseShare Account Deletion.

Request account deletion ↗

Send the request from your registered email address and include your ExpenseShare name. If you cannot access that address, contact support so we can verify ownership. Never send passwords, UPI PINs or verification codes. After verifying your request, support will remove your authentication account and associated personal profile and device-registration records. You can also delete your sign-in account from Settings → Delete account in the app, and use this email route to request removal of associated records.

Shared group expenses, settlements, messages and member references may be retained so other members can continue using their records; account deletion does not automatically erase these shared records. This shared information has no automatic expiry and remains until removed through the group's available controls. If a verified deletion request cannot be fully completed because of shared records or an applicable retention requirement, support will explain what remains and why. There is no automatic 90-day deletion policy.
